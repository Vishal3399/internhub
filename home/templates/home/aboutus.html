A better beginning

Connecting students with career opportunities.

InternHub brings students and companies together to make the first step into a professional career more accessible.

InternHub is an internship platform designed to connect students with companies and help students take their first step toward a professional career.

Students can discover internships, build their profiles, upload resumes, and apply for opportunities. Companies can create their profiles, post internship opportunities, and find students with relevant skills.

What InternHub offers

Tools for every step of the internship journey, for both students and the companies looking to meet them.

For students

  • Create a professional student profile
  • Add education, skills, and projects
  • Upload resumes and explore internships
  • Apply for opportunities and track progress
  • Save interesting internship opportunities

For companies

  • Create a company profile
  • Post internship opportunities
  • View student applications
  • Review candidate profiles and resumes
  • Find students based on their skills

How it works

A few straightforward steps can bring students and employers closer together.

01 / PROFILE

Create an account

Students and companies create their respective accounts.

02 / PREPARE

Build your profile

Share your education, skills, resume, or company details.

03 / DISCOVER

Find opportunities

Explore internships that fit your skills and interests.

04 / CONNECT

Apply or hire

Students apply and companies meet motivated candidates.
